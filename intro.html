ThornesTowne

ThornesTowne, a small remote village. Surrounded by luscious green fields, it is nestled by the Crystal Rivers, which wrap around the Prickling Woods of the east province of Agraphyll. Despite being a small village, it's a bustling town that sees more traffic than most like it. ThornesTowne acts as a rest and stock-up point for most passing by, making most of its revenue off the plentiful amount of crops that are grown and their high quality. Wheat, corn, and bagel-nuts, are just a few among the choices to pick from.

The residents of ThornesTowne are just as friendly as the crops are good. People greet each other and passerby's with cheerful hellos and smiles and food baskets for their neighbors. You often walk through the town, carrying your crops to shops and making deliveries.

After training for some time, you decide that it's finally time to go out and begin your adventure. You bid your farewells to your parents and turn around and leave your childhood home. Walking through the town one last time, you also bid your farewells to your fellow neighbors and residents, chatting happily before you move onto the next house. After making your last trip around ThornesTowne, you walk towards the town gates. You take one last look behind you, taking in as much of your old home to remember as you can. With a heavy heart and a burning passion, you turn around and take your first step outside into the world.

As you step your first steps outside the gates of ThornesTowne, you take a breath of fresh air and look around you in excitement. The tall, green grass reaches high above the ground, blocking most of what you can see besides the narrow dirt road. The sounds of of rushing water from the Crystal Waters river can be heard off in the distance. The scenery is beautiful until your eyes land on the Prickling Woods. Large, black and bare trees seem to reach towards the clouds as you gaze into the forest. So many trees are packed tightly together all you can see is a few feet into the forest before the rest of it is replaced with pure darkness. You think you can hear sounds of animals, or something else, but you can't make out exactly what. With a soft shudder, you turn your attention back to the main road and continue on your way.

After what seems like hours of walking, you stumble upon a humble gnome who offers you a potion out of his little pouch on his belt. Before you can respond, he empties his bag in front of you and out poors a seemingly endless amount of what looks to be the same potion type. You can't tell what it is by it's color, and the gnome refuses to elabortate. You can take the potion, or as many as you would like to.
