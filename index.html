Welcome to An Adventurer's Quest!

Many from far and wide come to the land of [Agraphyll] for many reasons. Honor, fame, riches, anything is said to be possible in this world of possibilities. Be wary, though, for every chance at success, there is an equal chance of failure. The land is full of dangers, from unpredictable climates to fierce monsters. You can never be too safe when trying to find your way.

You're a local who grew up in Thornestowne, a small, remote village nestled by the Crystal Rivers, which wrap around the Prickling (or Twisting) Woods of the east province of [Agraphyll]. You lived on a small plot of land, your parents providing by working the farm and some odd jobs around town. Once old enough, you also began helping with the farm. You were content with your nice, cozy farmer's life, until one day you began longing for something more, something more thrilling and “unknown”. You decided you wanted to find yourself, and you knew you couldn’t do that by staying in ThornesTown. You decide to head out on your own, aiming to reach the capital of the east province, Lunevaria (pronounced “loon-var-ee-uh”), a bustling seaport city off the coast of the Lunar Oceans.

Hearing of the dangers beyond your hometown, you first decide that you should prepare and train before leaving though. The size of your town limits your options, though. You could train in the art of the sword, the arcane, or the shadows. After some thought, you made your choice.
