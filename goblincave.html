Goblin Cave

Shaking your head, you turn away from the emanating light and the alluring sound of the humming. You walk forwards and towards the cave entrance as the sound of the humming becomes fainter and fainter before you can no longer hear it.

Stepping up to the cave's entrance, you find a smouldering fire. Somebody was just here. Surrounding the fire are destroyed wooden boxes and other debris. You quickly gather that there was some kind of commotion that took place here. You look up at the mouth of the cave. You can't see anything but pure darkness. As you squint your eyes trying to see something, anything, you hear a loud cackling noise followed by a chorus of harsh laughter.
