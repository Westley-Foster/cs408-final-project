Fae's Waterfall

You decide that finding out what the source of the humming is is more important than investigating the goblin cave. You turn and move forward towards the glowing light. The sound of the humming increases and feels more alluring the closer that you get. Nearly tripping over the thick vines, you finally reach the bushes to which the light is emanating from. You begin to brush them aside and step forward, covering your eyes with your hand as the light intensifies.

Stepping past the bushes, the ligh begins to dim and you lower your hand from your eyes. Before you is a small clearing with short and dark green grass. Trees a beautiful soft brown surround the enclave on all sides. Towards the back of the clearing is a small waterhole surrounded by medium-sized stones, which in turn are have darker green bushes at the bases of each stone. A small stone sits in the centr of the water with a great waterfall mystically flows gently into the pool, the water being so crystal clear that it looks like an illusion. After stepping closer to the water, you notice a few small dots of light hovering above the middle stone.

First, there's silence as you stare at the lights. Then, you hear a soft hum emanate from the lights. A tune so beautiful and soft that even the nearby forest critters stop to listen. You yourself can't help but stand and listen to the gently song as well. A soft ray shines above on the humming dots, acting as a spotlight. After some time, you find yourself walking towards the center stone, wanting to get a better look at what the humming lights could be.

As you inch closer to the waterhole, you can make out a few small figures inside the balls of light. They're a small group of fairies that fly and dance side-to-side in sync with their serenade. You take a step closer, but suddenly hear a loud *crunch* beneath your feet. The fairies' song comes to a sudden stop as they turn their attention to you. Then, they quickly disperse and fly away in all directions, disappearing behind the trees of the forest. You look down and see a broken twig. Swearing to yourself in annoyance, you begin walking towards the waterhole where the fairies sang and gaze into the crystal water before turning and leaving the enclave.

You push past the bright light of the bushes once more, and find yourself back in the open fields. After your eyes adjust to the light, you turn around and see the glowing light slowly fade away as the dark black trees take its place. You turn back around and see the goblin's cave off in the distance. With a deep breath, you begin walking towards the cave.
