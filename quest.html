The Outer Fields

In front of you lies a seemingly endless field of grass and flowers. You look up and towards bright blue sky, admiring the puffy white clouds float by as the sun brightens the fields in front of you with a warm glow. You lower your head and breathe a sigh of relaxation. You continue on your journey, the grass and flowers swaying gently in the wind as you follow the dirt road towards Lunevaria.

You hear the shuffling of your bushes to your left. Turning your head to the movement, you see the Prickling Woods in its eerie glory. The woods are more grand than you could ever imagine. Dark-colored trees as wide as two horse-drawn carriages reach endlessly toward the sky, their tops only being visible when one squints hard. The trees are packed tightly together, blocking almost all sunlight from entering the forest. You try peering into the woods, but can only see a few feet in before the darkness overtakes everything, leaving you to only imagine what sleeps in the forest.

Your attention turns back towards the bushes as the rustling becomes harsher. Suddenly, the bushes stop shaking as you notice something burst from the bushes, running through the tall grass in front of you and towards you. The tall grass obscures whatever it is, but you can see it moving closer as the grass flattens beneath its feet. You begin to back up, your nervousness ever increasing as the mysterious creature draws nearer.

Before you can back up too much, though, the creature emerges from the grass and stop in front of you. You take a sigh of relief as you notice that it's a small warthog. It's a young one, so it poses little danger to you. You smile and begin to approach the warthog. You kneel down to pet it, but before you can it dash around you and runs away in a hurry.

Watching the hog depart, you feel a tinge of disappointment. You turn back around towards the Prickling Woods, but find yourself face-to-face with a large, growling wolf. You desperately back up in sudden fear, but fall down as you attempt to run. The wolf slowly approaches you as you scoot away in hurry. It barks as it lunges at you.

Thankfully, you roll out of the way and get to your feet before it lands on top of you. You sprint away as fast your legs can move. You turn your head and see the wolf giving chase, and it's catching up fast. It gets behind you and lunges once more. Ducking down and covering your head with your arms as you close your eyes, you hear a sudden *thunk* in front of you, before a short whine is heard as the wolf falls to the ground mid-air.

You turn your head to the wolf and see it lying on the ground with an arrow stuck in its side. It's whimpering, so it's still alive. You snap your head back and see a small man standing before you, still holding his longbow in shooting position. He lowers his hands and looks at you. “Well, hello there, stranger!”, he bellows in a deep, booming voice. “That sure was a close one. You need to be more careful out here. Despite its looks, these fields are dangerous”.

He sheathes his bow behind his back and takes a few steps towards you. Extending his hand, he greets himself. "The name's Bueford". He grabs your hand and squeezes so hard you think your hand is about to fall off. You introduce yourself back to him and get a better look at him now that things are calmed down. His face is grizzled and dirty with a large, orange beard gripping the sides of his face. It seems to you that he's a dwarf. After a moment, he releases your hand and takes a step back. "Now what're you doing here sonny? Itching for an adventure no doubt".

You explain to the small dwarf why you're here on your journey and tell him that you came out to find yourself and yes, adventure too. "Ah, sounds like a good tale for ye!", he bellows once more. "Well, I'm running out of time and it looks like you might need yourself a better weapon. I got just the thing for ye". He reaches down to his belt and tugs at the small bag on his waste. It's purple with a golden lace keeping it tied closed. He opens it and begins digging around inside of it.

"No no, that won't do...", he mumbles to himself as he digs through the small bag. It's then you realize that that small bag sounds like there's something big in it. Just then, he pulls out a large wooden box closed shut with metal hinges. The wood looks rough like it's seen some tough years, but it still holds strong. "Ah!", he exclaims, "here it is! This here is a box of desired items. You simply touch the boxes handle and imagine what you want and then the box will open, revealing the item you desired. There's no limit either, so go on, give it a try!"

You take a step forward, a little skeptical and apprehensive of the dwarf's description. Nevertheless, you decide what's the worst that can happen? You reach your hand forward and touch the box.

What item or weapon would you like to create?

After you take your items, the box closes itself with a soft *thud*. You give your thanks to Bueford as he lifts the wonderous box up and shoves it back into the tiny bag. After strapping the purple bag back to his belt, he looks up to you and waves his hand side-to-side. "No thanks needed, lad. You should be better equipped to survive out here now", he says. "I best be getting on my way too, I don't wanna linger in the same place for too long". As he turns arounds, he stops about halfway before he continues to speak. "Oh, I do have a small favor to ask in return though".

He pulls out a map from his pocket and unfolds it, displaying the drawing to you. "I was given a job to clear out a cave of goblins near here". He points to a small dot on the map with a circle around it. "However," he says, "I'm in need of a good drink, and considering you're well-equipped now, you mind doing it for me?". Before you can answer, he shoves the map into your hands and quickly stands up. He turns around and starts running towards ThornesTowne as he looks back and waves at you "Thanks mate!".

You stand there, stunned, as you watch the little man disappear behind the tall grass in the distance. Glancing back at the map, you think to yourself that this could be good experience to gain before continuing on your adventure. You stuff the map into your pocket and start walking further down the road.

After some time, you eventually come to a point in the road where you can barely make out the opening of a small cave, with a dying fire near the mouth of the cave. You look down at the map and realize that this is the cave Bueford was talking about. You take a few steps forward towards the cave, but stop in your tracks as you hear a faint sound from behind you. It sounds like...humming?

Turning around, you see a small, beaten path almost entirely overgrown with thick green vines leading into the woods. A faint glow of white light is emitting from the brush beyond the path. The humming seems to be coming from somewhere behidn the light. You feel a strange sensation of calmness and relaxation as you hear the tune repeatedly play. You take a step forward before stopping yourself. You still need to take care of the goblins in the cave, but you also feel like you need to investigate the source of the humming. Do you investigate the humming, or do you investigate the goblin cave?
